## What changed vs 17C/17D (measurement-model correction)

`run_ekf_case` (17C/17D) fed every GNSS/course update the ground-truth reference position regardless of arm, so a switching decision's quality could not show up in FDE. `run_ekf_case_v2` (17E) feeds the scaffold's actual `reported_gnss_x/y` instead -- noisy during DEGRADED, frozen during OUTAGE -- for every arm including a re-baselined ORACLE_REPORTED. This is why 17E's numbers are not comparable to 17C's/17D's raw FDE values.

## Limitations

- The 'validation-only' cases (Section 15) are non-overlapping windows on the same 4 test sequences, not genuine validation-split sequences -- flagged, not hidden; a production run should use real validation-split data.
- `InnovationAdaptiveController`'s trust-vs-EMA function is a simple linear ramp, not fit/learned -- a smoother or learned mapping was out of scope here.
- Synthetic GNSS-quality scaffold throughout (carried over from 17B/17C).
