## What this notebook is and is not

- IS: a fixed, orthogonal ablation (`course_enabled` on/off) applied identically to every arm from 17E -- 8 combinations total, no continuous parameter swept, nothing fit to the test set.
- IS NOT: a fix for the course update itself (e.g. a noise-adaptive baseline threshold) -- if COURSE_OFF wins cleanly, that is evidence FOR building such a fix, but the fix itself is a distinct, explicitly-scoped follow-up (17G), not silently added here.

## What's reused verbatim (frozen, not re-tuned here)

- `EKF5_CV` (all methods, incl. `peek_gnss_nis`), v3 robustness constants, Q/P0/SIG_GNSS_M
- `GNSSHealthMonitor` + thresholds, GNSS scaffold (same seed)
- Frozen 72-case outage list, Phase-16 winner, `NHC_STD_MPS`
- `GNSSSwitchController` (17D's tuned constants), `InnovationAdaptiveController` (17E's tuned constants, loaded not re-derived)

## Limitations

- Synthetic GNSS-quality scaffold throughout (carried over from 17B/17C/17E).
- This ablation is binary (course fully on or fully off); a noise-adaptive middle ground was deliberately left out of scope to keep this notebook a clean diagnostic rather than a new source of test-set-fit tuning.

## STOP

17F complete (subject to the checklist below). If COURSE_OFF wins for ORACLE_REPORTED and/or the other arms, a noise-adaptive course-update gate (replacing the fixed COG_BASELINE_M) is the natural, explicitly-scoped 17G follow-up -- not something to bolt on here.
